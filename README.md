# NYC-schools-admission-analysis
## Project Overview
This project analyzes SAT performance across New York City (NYC) public high schools using the dataset schools.csv, which includes average scores for Reading, Math, and Writing—each scored out of 800 points. Because SAT performance plays a significant role in U.S. college admissions, understanding how schools perform is valuable for educators, policymakers, researchers, and families.
The goal of this analysis is to uncover patterns in school-level SAT outcomes, identify top and low performers, and explore factors that may be associated with academic performance across the city.
## Objectives
This project answers three key questions:
What is the overall SAT performance of NYC public schools across Reading, Math, and Writing?
Analyze score distributions
Compare performance across sections
## Identify general trends
Which schools perform the best and worst on the SAT?
Rank schools by total average SAT score
Highlight top and bottom performers
Which factors may be linked to differences in SAT scores?
Examine participation rate
Explore demographic and school-level characteristics
Identify potential relationships or disparities
